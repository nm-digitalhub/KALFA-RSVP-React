Consulting FAQ

## Questions teams ask before scoping

-   How much does Workflow Builder consulting cost?
    
    Consulting layers on top of an Enterprise Edition license (EUR 6,990). A Workflow Setup Sprint runs EUR 15,000-25,000 for a 2-4 week kickoff. End-to-end delivery is custom-scoped. Everything up to the ballpark proposal - intro call, Discovery sales call, and the proposal itself - is free of charge.
    
-   What's the difference between the Setup Sprint and End-to-end services?
    
    Sprint = we help you start, you finish. End-to-end = we deliver to production. Sprint suits teams with engineering capacity who want a head start; End-to-end suits teams who want a partner to ship the workflow feature while their engineers focus on the rest of the product.
    
-   How do you transfer knowledge to our team?
    
    Pairing from day 1, AI-assisted workflow setup, purpose-built Claude skills for your codebase, scaffolding scripts, and documentation. We are not building dependencies - we transfer capability. Your team owns the code when we leave.
    
-   Can Workflow Builder integrate with our existing React Flow canvas?
    
    Usually yes, though not as a drop-in layer over your existing canvas. We typically (a) rebuild your current UX on the Workflow Builder SDK, (b) contribute to your codebase, or (c) plan a migration from React Flow. Our Workflow architecture consulting track identifies the right path.
    
-   What if our team has React experience but not workflow editors?
    
    **Common case. The Setup Sprint includes architecture explanation plus pairing. After 2-4 weeks your team owns it. We have onboarded React teams to workflow editor patterns in dozens of engagements.**
    
-   Do you do Workflow Builder consulting for regulated industries (fintech, healthcare, defense)?
    
    Yes. NDA, SOC2-aligned and GDPR-compliant processes, we can work in your environment. 200+ workflow products delivered across regulated verticals - including BMW (industrial), TotalEnergies (energy), and others under NDA.
    
-   Can we start with a workflow feasibility study and expand later?
    
    Yes. The workflow feasibility study is one of our five consulting tracks - you can engage it standalone, then expand into a Setup Sprint or End-to-end engagement if the path becomes clear.
