## What Workflow Builder does

Workflow Builder is a **white-label React SDK** designed for software teams that need to embed a visual workflow editor into their product – without months of custom development. Rather than building drag-and-drop canvases, node libraries, property panels, and execution logic from the ground up, development teams integrate Workflow Builder and begin customizing immediately.

The result: **production-ready workflow UI in days, not months** – with full ownership of the frontend source code, no vendor lock-in, and a design system built for enterprise-grade customization.

The SDK is trusted by engineering teams at major enterprises, industrial conglomerates, and prominent scale-ups across North America, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region. One early adopter – a publicly traded communications platform – reported **saving approximately €50,000** and several months of engineering time by adopting Workflow Builder instead of building in-house.

Workflow Builder is available in two editions: a **Community Edition** (open source, free) for developers exploring workflow UI concepts, and an **Enterprise Edition** (one-time perpetual license) for organizations building production systems that require advanced features, dedicated support, and long-term scalability.

## About Workflow Builder and the company behind it

Workflow Builder is a production-ready React SDK for embedding visual workflow editors into software products. Built by experts in diagram-based interfaces, it provides a scalable canvas, node library, dynamic properties panels, auto-layout, flow execution logic, and a Figma design system – out of the box. Teams can customize every layer using design tokens and React components, connecting the editor to any backend architecture.

Workflow Builder is a product of **Synergy Codes**, a Wrocław-based company with over 15 years of experience in diagram-based solutions and data visualization. Synergy Codes delivers workflow and automation interfaces to clients ranging from VC-backed startups to Fortune 500 corporations across North America, Europe, and Asia.
