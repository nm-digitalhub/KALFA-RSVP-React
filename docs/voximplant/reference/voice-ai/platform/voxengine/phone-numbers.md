To make and receive calls to stationary or mobile numbers, you need a phone number attached to your application. This number is essential for incoming calls and caller ID for outgoing calls.

Buying a phone number

To buy a phone number, log in to your Voximplant control panel and select the Numbers section from the main menu:

In the left upper corner, choose Buy a new phone number:

This opens a list of available phone numbers for purchase.

You can choose between real phone numbers and test ones, and specify the desired country, state, and city:

Test phone numbers are invaluable for testing applications and developers. To make a call to a test phone number, dial one of Voximplant’s inner numbers and enter it as an extension.

Please note

You cannot use a test phone number as your Caller ID.

Once you purchase a phone number, you can attach it to your application and utilize it.

Attaching a phone number to an application

To attach a number to your application, navigate to the Applications section in your control panel, select the relevant application, and switch to the Numbers tab. Choose an available number and click Attach:

While attaching the number, you have the option to set a routing rule or do it manually later.

Note

You can purchase multiple numbers and assign them to multiple routing rules to process them in different ways.

Once you attach a phone number to your application, you can make and receive calls.
